CellTip

real on-chain tipping Nervos CKB · Pudge testnet

Connect a real wallet, deposit real testnet CKB into a zero-knowledge rollup pool, tip creators in tiny amounts off-chain, and withdraw back to your own wallet. Deposits and withdrawals are real proven checkpoints on Pudge (each takes a few minutes to prove); tips in between are instant. Your money stays yours, the operator can never take it or redirect a withdrawal.

Step 1 · Connect your wallet

CellTip uses JoyID (a passkey wallet, no extension needed). You will also get a one-time in-browser session key that signs your tips, it never leaves this device, so only you can move your pool funds.

Step 2 · Tip creators (instant, off-chain)

Tips are signed by your session key and held off-chain, instant and feeless to you (a 0.001 CKB protocol fee accrues to the pool). They are committed on-chain at your next checkpoint (a deposit, withdrawal, or "settle").

Step 3 · Cash out to your wallet

A proven checkpoint pays an output cell to your own JoyID lock, the CKB lands back in your wallet. You signed the exact target with your session key, so the operator cannot send it anywhere else. Minimum ~63 CKB (output cell floor).

Is my money safe?

Even if this app vanished, your balance is reconstructable from the published on-chain data and you hold a unilateral escape hatch. The operator only orders and proves, it can never steal, inflate, or freeze your funds, and a withdrawal is bound to a lock you signed. That is enforced by the anchor type script that verifies every checkpoint's zk-proof on a live CKB block.
